Walnut’s Digital Sales Room (aka the Interactive Deal Room) is where a deal stops being a PDF-on-email and becomes an actual experience. It wraps the whole buyer journey into one clean, flexible space that helps Sales enable and Delivery deliver, without the “where’s the latest version?” scavenger hunt. Built lean, tested hard, and designed to scale.
